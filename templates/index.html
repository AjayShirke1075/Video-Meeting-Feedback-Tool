InterviewIQ
How it works Presentation review

Local practice tool. Not a public product.

Practice a job interview against your resume and the posting you applied to.

You upload a resume and a job description. The app writes questions for that role, records your face and voice while you answer, then scores each answer and writes a report you can export as PDF.

How a session runs

  1. Intake. Upload or paste a resume and a job description (PDF, DOCX, or TXT, 16 MB max).
  2. Questions. Seven questions are generated from that pair. Demo mode uses a fallback set if no Gemini key is set.
  3. Answers. Type or dictate. Webcam and microphone feed facial expression and voice volume into the score.
  4. Report. Per-question scores, session totals, and a PDF export. Nothing is stored after the session ends.

What is measured

        GUIDELINES

        Voluntary Practice & Ethical Principles

        Commitment to honest coaching without psychological claims

        1

        Observable Cues Are Not Internal Emotions

        Visual presentation analysis tracks outward facial cues, gaze steadiness, and smile frequencies. It never claims to read a person’s internal feelings, authenticity, sincerity, or character.

        2

        Voluntary Self-Coaching Only

        This tool is built solely for speakers practicing voluntarily on their own terms. It must never be used for involuntary workplace monitoring, surveillance, or automated evaluation.

        3

        Honoring Natural & Cultural Baselines

        Different cultures, personality temperaments, and neurodivergent individuals exhibit different baseline facial expressions. Metrics measure personal relative trajectory, not a standardized behavioral mould.

        4

        100% Client-Side Privacy

        Video frames, audio streams, and notes remain inside your local browser session. No recordings are retained or transmitted to external third-party servers.
